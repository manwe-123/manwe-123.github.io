Somewhere between the general body meetings and the late-night event setups, I made my closest friends at ASU. They are the people I study with, the people I ask for advice, and the people who push me to be a better engineer. For a Hispanic student in a field where we are underrepresented, that kind of community is not a luxury. It is the thing that makes the degree feel attainable." - title: "Connecting Culture and Engineering" description: "SHPE is also where my heritage and my profession stopped being separate parts of my life. Events like Noche de Ciencias bring science and engineering directly to Hispanic families and students, showing kids that someone who looks like them and speaks like them does this work. Being part of that mission has connected me more deeply to my culture than any other part of college. It is also the reason I wanted to lead outreach rather than any other role on the board." - title: "Outreach Director" description: "As Outreach Director, I own the chapter's relationship with the community outside campus. I plan and coordinate at least eight outreach, volunteer, and professional development events per year, including Noche de Ciencias and our signature Engineers Week event. I build partnerships with local schools, prioritizing those with large Hispanic student populations, and coordinate with community organizations to bring them in. I manage a student events committee, a volunteer roster, and an event budget, and I design hands-on technical workshops that give members practical engineering skills. The job is mostly logistics, communication, and follow-through. The payoff is watching a student realize engineering is something people like them actually do." - title: "Finance Committee: Marketing Chair" description: "Before joining the executive board, I served on the Finance Committee as Marketing Chair from January to March 2026. I supported the promotion of chapter events and learned how SHPE operates from the inside: how budgets get set, how events get approved, and how much unglamorous work happens before a single attendee walks in. That semester taught me the internal mechanics of the chapter and prepared me to step into the Outreach Director role when the opportunity came." - title: "Networking and Professional Growth" description: "SHPE has also been my fastest route into the professional engineering world. Through chapter events and professional mixers, I have connected with many engineers working in industry who gave me honest advice about internships, graduate school, and what the job is actually like. Those conversations shaped how I approach my own career planning. Combined with the leadership experience of running outreach, SHPE has developed me professionally in ways that coursework alone cannot." - title: "What I Take With Me" description: "When people ask why I spend so much time on SHPE, the honest answer is that it gives me the same thing I hope our outreach events give a high schooler: proof that I belong in this field. The friends, the mentors, and the cultural connection are the reason I can walk into a recruiting event or a plant floor and feel at home." ---
