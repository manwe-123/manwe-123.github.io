From the Hive, IDIA's community learning space, we address that gap directly by teaching skills, distributing devices, and meeting people exactly where they are." - title: What I Do description: As a Digital Navigator, I instruct over 300 visitors every week in hardware and software fundamentals. Some are older adults learning to video call their families. Some are students building their first 3D model in TinkerCAD. Some are job seekers formatting a resume on a device they have never touched. I develop and deliver structured curriculum for nine distinct platforms, including Swift, TinkerCAD, and SpheroEDU, and I operate and maintain the center's Flashforge 3D printers, troubleshooting hardware failures and tuning print parameters in Orca Slicer to keep output consistent. - title: "Why This Work Matters to Me" description: "This is the part that does not fit on a resume. I joined IDIA in January 2025, and it remains the most fulfilling work I have ever done. Watching someone's face change when they finally understand a device, or when a printed part comes off the bed clean, genuinely brings me joy. I love this job because it is proof that technology only matters when people can actually use it. That belief shapes the kind of engineer I intend to become: one who builds things people can reach, understand, and rely on." - title: "The Engineering Connection" description: "The Hive is also where I practice engineering in public. Those habits transfer directly to mechanical design, fabrication, and plant operations." - title: "By the Numbers" description: "Nearly two years of continuous service, January 2025 to Present 300+ visitors instructed every week across all age groups 3D printers maintained and operated Nine software platforms with structured, standardized curriculum Thousands of hands-on technology interactions facilitated" ---
