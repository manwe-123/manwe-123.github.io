About Me

Mechanical engineering student focused on design, fabrication, and making ideas real.

Biography

I am currently a Mechanical Engineering student at Arizona State University with a focus on practical, hands-on execution. My path in engineering is driven by a desire to understand how complex physical systems operate and how to build mechanisms that solve real-world problems. Whether I am working through CAD modeling in SolidWorks, wiring microcontrollers, or writing custom Python data pipelines from scratch, I enjoy bridging the gap between theoretical calculations and functional tools.

At the center of my work is a core commitment to sustainability and environmental design. I want to develop technology that works in harmony with natural resources rather than depleting them, whether that means optimizing energy systems, reducing waste, or exploring smart urban infrastructure. After my EPICS in Spain experience, the concept of urban innovation, particularly how cities can be designed to be more walk-friendly, resource-efficient, and human-centered, strongly shapes the direction I want to take my career and my future graduate studies in UPC Barcelona.

Outside of engineering coursework and technical projects, I maintain a set of active, hands-on hobbies that keep me balanced. I play guitar and piano, spend time sewing and customizing garments or functional gear, and train in boxing during the week. These activities challenge me in different ways, requiring physical discipline, creative patience, and attention to rhythm and detail. They serve as a constant reminder that the process of refining a skill takes time, focus, and iteration.

Ultimately, my goal is to live a life centered on meaningful experiences, travel, and genuine impact rather than pure material gain. I want to use my education to build solutions that help people and protect the environment, whether through designing sustainable hardware, teaching, or working on community-driven technical projects. As I work toward completing my degree at ASU and preparing for CSWA certification, I remain focused on growing into a adaptable engineer capable of making a positive, lasting contribution.
