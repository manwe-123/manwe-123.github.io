About Me

Mechanical engineering student focused on design, fabrication, and making ideas real.

Biography

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at Arizona State University focused on robotics, fabrication, and product development. I enjoy projects that start as an idea, move through CAD and analysis, and end with a physical prototype that can be tested and improved.

My interest in mechanical systems comes from hands-on work in the shop and on team-based engineering projects. That experience has strengthened my problem-solving, communication, and technical skills while showing me how much thoughtful design matters in the real world.

I am especially interested in opportunities that combine CAD, manufacturing, and testing. My goal is to keep building experience that helps me design practical systems and understand the full process from concept to implementation.

Outside of class, I like staying involved in technical and creative projects that keep me learning. I am always happy to connect with people interested in engineering, design, collaboration, and making useful things.
